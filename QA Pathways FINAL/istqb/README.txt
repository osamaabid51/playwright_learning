Put your own copies of the official ISTQB files here (personal use), then reload the app:

  syllabus.pdf    - ISTQB CTFL Syllabus v4.0.1, free download from istqb.org
                    (https://istqb.org/certifications/certified-tester-foundation-level-ctfl-v4-0/)
                    Lessons then show "Syllabus p. N" links that open the page.
  questions.json  - the CTFL Prep official question pack (Sample Exams A-D),
                    the same file the CTFL Prep app used in its istqb folder.
                    Mock tests then offer ISTQB Sample Exam A-D (40 questions, 60 min).

Exam dates come from the Pakistan Software Testing Board (pstb.pk) while the app is running.
